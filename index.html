My Love,

I have no idea how and when you become such a constant part of my mind. At some point, I think, talking to you stopped feeling like something I simply looked forward to and started feeling like a part of my day was missing when you weren't there. When I started talking to you, I started noticing things just because I would think about telling you about them. I just wanted you to know them all.

I have always imagined falling in love like this. There is something beautiful about loving someone that way... you quietly begin to exist in parts of my life you've never even physically been in.

You are so far away from me, Ashley, but somehow you have made yourself at home in my days. I wish you knew how often I think about ordinary things with you. I think about that a lot, actually. All the small things I wish distance didn’t keep me from doing. I want to know what you look like when you’ve just woken up and you’re still half asleep. I want to sit next to you while you’re doing something completely ordinary. I want to hear you complain about something stupid that happened during your day. I want to make you food and probably annoy you while you’re trying to eat it lol. I want the kind of moments that nobody would ever think to photograph.

And I know there are probably moments when everything feels uncertain. When the distance feels enormous, when meeting feels complicated, when you wonder whether you’re ready, whether I’ll still feel the same when that day finally comes.

So, I want you to know this very clearly.

You never have to hurry for me, and that there is no clock on us. And that you do NOT owe me a certain version of yourself before I can love you. You don't have to become more confident, more comfortable, more put together, or more anything... before I see you. I want to see you because you are you.

And whenever that day comes, maybe sooner than we imagine, or maybe after we've complained about distance a million times, I know that I am going to be the happiest person on earth that day simply because the person standing in front of me will finally be the girl i've spent all the nights talking to. The girl whose voice I die to hear. Whose laugh is so contagious and so full of life. The girl whose touch I miss so much without even having had a chance to hold her yet.

It's strange, isn't it? To miss someone in ways you haven't even experienced yet.. I miss sitting beside you, I miss holding your hand, I miss resting my head against yours, and I miss looking at you from too close. None of the experiences exist yet but somehow my heart has already made a place for them. But also, I don’t want you to think that I spend every day impatiently waiting for some future version of us to begin.

Because this is us too. Stupid conversations, 18hr long calls, misunderstandings, flirting, you looking for porn while we watch movies... They matter to me "too much".

Sometimes I catch myself thinking about how unlikely all of this was. There are billions of people scattered across the world, living entirely separate lives, and somehow you ended up becoming someone I care about this deeply. And I think there is still more to you than you choose to let out. I am trying to know more of you slowly every day.

I don’t know exactly what our future will look like. I don’t want to make enormous promises. But I can promise you something smaller and, I think, more meaningful. While we are building this, I will keep showing up honestly. And like I said, I will never want you to feel like you have to earn my patience or rush yourself because you’re afraid I might disappear. I am always here.

What else..? Yes, I wish I could shrink the world a little enough so that we didn't need planes or passports to be together. Just enough that when I say, "I'm here", I could mean it literally. But well, I cannot shrink the world, I can just try to be with you, and one day we will be together. Until then, I hope you know I mean it in every way that I can.

I’m here.

I miss you.

I want you close.

I love you.

I love you more than I can fit into this little page. And apparently I’ve already tried.

Always yours,
Ash ♡
