# For Ash: where the surprises are

This guide is not linked from Ashley's page. Leave it out of the GitHub upload if you want the spoilers to stay local.

## The room

Tap the empty seat to sit beside Ash. The **↻** stamp changes the weather. Keyboard users can tab through objects and use Enter or Space.

| Surprise | How to find it |
| --- | --- |
| Ashley's Miku drawing | Open the little wooden drawer to reveal her full landscape drawing, including both green characters. |
| Her new Shin Chan cup | Tap the green cup on the side table. Her actual photo opens in a taped frame. |
| Daily rug trail | Read the three stitched symbols across the rug, then tap the matching room objects in that order. The clock, picture, window, lamp, plant, drawer, cushion and cup can appear in the combination. The random sequence stays the same through room changes and reloads for that day. Correct taps fill the patches; a wrong tap gently resets the progress. There are no sound effects. Completing it opens today’s folded sticker. Each of 365 designs appears once; no note accompanies it. |
| Her collection | Tap the small green fridge. Tap a sticker to enlarge it and see its date. Stored in that browser, without cross-device sync. |
| Warm light | Tap the lamp. The lighting changes inside the room without opening anything. |
| Window skies | Tap the window to cycle through fireworks spelling **I ♡ YOU**, a meteor shower, a shooting star and wish, an aurora, floating heart lanterns, and a sleepy moon cloud. |
| 6–7 clock | Tap the clock. The hour hand points to 6 and the minute hand points to 7. |
| Keep the plant alive | Water the plant once a day. It becomes thirsty after two days and brown and wilted after three days without water. One tap revives it, and the last-watered date persists in that browser. |
| Ashley's cat drawing | Tap the drawing frame at the upper right. One of her two actual cat drawings is used for that room and opens larger when tapped. |
| MUTE ASH remote | Lift the little cushion. It moves aside and a remote marked **MUTE ASH** appears beside it. Ash’s face changes while muted and “oh come on” fades away. Press the remote again to lower the cushion, restore his usual expression and get one of four fading unmuted replies. |
| Blanket for two | Tap the folded pink blanket near the lower-left edge of the room. Ashley sits down if needed, the bears move closer without sliding down, and the blanket tucks around both of them. Tap the basket in the same spot to fold it back. |
| A little closer | Tap Ash's bear. Ashley joins him if she hasn't sat down; the bears lean closer with a burst of hearts. |

The carpet has a yarn ball, a bunny plush and the daily stitched clue patches. There are no carpet mini games, joke menus or room sound effects. Only Ashley's supplied Miku drawing, cat drawing and new Shin Chan cup open as room image pop-ups. The Rilakkuma photo is not repeated there. Only the sticker collection is limited to one new sticker per local day.

## Find it

Six settings shuffle without repeating until the deck is exhausted. Each round deals six objects from its setting, a new clue sequence and one of seven doodles. The objects below won't all appear together; replay to find another setting.

| Surprise | How to find it |
| --- | --- |
| Hidden heart message | Tap **Heart sticker** five times. Every fifth tap reveals a shuffled small secret. |
| Slayed potato / aloo | **my controller** → **Read the player tag**. Tap the controller itself three times for an achievement. The nickname refers to Ash, not Ashley. |
| Unsent messages | **an unlocked phone** → **Read the unsent message**. Three taps on the phone reveal the typing-and-deleting exchange. No monkey jokes here. |
| Language lesson | **a pocket dictionary** → **Read the pencilled word** for benchod. |
| Uncle toilet on duty | **a rubber duck** → **Look underneath**. Diaper duties, a family group chat and Ashley volunteering Ash for a shift. Tap the duck itself three times for the emergency pager joke. These are playful imagined situations. |
| Your way of speaking | **a scribbled voice memo** → **What was I saying?** for “oh come on” and “too much”. Written exchanges, not audio. |
| Random drawing | **my notebook** → **Check the doodle**. One of seven: two Shin Chan drawings, stick figures, a bunny, an Ash + Ashley heart, or either of Ashley's two cats. Her cats get short reactions above the image. No captions beneath or manual next button. Reopening keeps the same drawing and reaction. |
| Feet fetish allegations | Tap **a stray sock** three times. |
| A flower | Tap **the little plant** three times. |
| Lock becomes a piano | Press the **music note** three times consecutively at the lock. Resets the partial code and shows a separate playful reaction. Enter the displayed code afterward to continue. |
| 67 | Shake the parcel six times, then once more. The heart seal becomes 67 on shake seven. |
| Cartoon “I love you” and kisses | Shake the parcel **eleven times**. The uploaded MP3 plays with kisses and hearts floating over the whole screen. **Hear it again** replays both. Opening the parcel stops them; switching away pauses playback. Reduced-motion users get gentle fades. |
| Project Hail Mary detour | **a folded ticket** → **Read the tiny print**, or **a popcorn bowl** → **Check the folded napkin**. |
| Clock's 67 joke | **a small clock** → **Check the alarm**. |
| Rilakkuma photo | **a sleepy bear** → **Look behind its ear**. |
| Crooked bow | **a crooked little bow** → **Untie the bow**. Gift-wrapping mishaps, without the Miku joke. |
| Back of the final note | Finish the hunt, then **There is something on the back…**. Shuffled private exchanges; the cartoon category uses the round's drawing. |

The final hunt letter begins with 😘 and has no name beneath it. When `daily-letter.js` contains today's date and at least one paragraph, that personal message appears. Otherwise the shuffled fallback letter appears. The loose-floorboard section has been removed.

No Easter egg is required to finish the hunt. Notes and joke pools eventually cycle; the 365 stickers do not repeat within a collection. Clearing browser storage starts a new collection. Audio plays only after a tap.
